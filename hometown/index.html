South Tangerang City

A satellite city and buffer zone for Jakarta in Banten Province that is rapidly developing as a modern residential, business, and planned commercial hub.

South Tangerang City is one of the largest satellite cities in the Greater Jakarta area and an essential part of Banten Province. Known as a modern residential area and a self-sustained planned business center, South Tangerang is growing rapidly into a modern city based on technology, creative economy, and environmentally conscious education.
